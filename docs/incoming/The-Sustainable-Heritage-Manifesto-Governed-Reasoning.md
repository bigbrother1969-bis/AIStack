# Governed Reasoning

## Principle

Architectural and engineering reasoning shall always be derived from governed knowledge.

Governed knowledge is the primary source of reasoning for every engineering decision.
Personal experience, memory, intuition, implementation details and AI knowledge may assist reasoning, but shall never replace the governed heritage.

Consequences:
- reasoning is reproducible;
- reasoning is explainable;
- reasoning is traceable;
- the governed heritage is the architectural SPOT.
