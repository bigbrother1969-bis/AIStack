# AIStack Future Operational Maturity Backlog

## Purpose

This backlog contains future resilience and operational maturity improvements.

These capabilities are intentionally postponed.

They are not required to build the current AIStack roadmap.

They become relevant once the Knowledge Operating System foundations and validation use cases are stable.

---

# Knowledge Recovery Procedure

## Objective

Enable reconstruction of AIStack from preserved knowledge artifacts after a major failure.

## Scope

Define and validate a recovery procedure based on:

- Git repositories;
- Context Bundles;
- documentation;
- configuration;
- exported knowledge artifacts.

## Expected Outcome

A new environment can progressively rebuild AIStack knowledge and context.
